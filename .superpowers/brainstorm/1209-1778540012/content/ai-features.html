AI Writing Assistant

How deep should AI go in the Studio? Each tier adds cost and complexity.

A

No AI — pure writing tool

Zero API costs. Appeals to writers who distrust AI-generated content. Differentiates from tools that bolt on AI awkwardly. You compete on craft features, not AI gimmicks.

B

Light AI — editing assistance only

Grammar/style suggestions, readability scoring, chapter summaries for your own reference. No "write for me." Low token usage = low cost. Premium-gated. Positioned as a writing coach, not a ghostwriter.

C

Full AI — generation + editing

Autocomplete, "continue writing," brainstorm ideas, rewrite scenes, character dialogue suggestions. High token usage. Must be premium-gated with usage limits or you'll bleed money. Matches what Sudowrite/NovelAI offer.
