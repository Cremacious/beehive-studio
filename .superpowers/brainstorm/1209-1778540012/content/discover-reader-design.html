Key design decisions

  • Discover is chapter-first, not just book-first — "New chapters today" feed rewards active writers and gives readers a reason to return daily. Same mechanic that keeps RoyalRoad readers hooked.
  • Reader view is distraction-free — clean typography, no sidebar, max-width column like a real book page. The engagement bar (likes, comments, bookmark) stays pinned at the bottom.
  • Follow button in the reader — the moment a reader is hooked on a chapter is the best time to prompt a follow.
  • Genre filter bar at the top of Discover — primary browse path, same as RoyalRoad's genre tags.
  • Ads appear in the reader view for free users — between chapters or as a banner below the engagement bar. Premium removes them entirely.
