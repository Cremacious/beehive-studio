Hive — Premium Perks

Core collaboration is free for everyone. Select which Hive features should be premium-only.

1

More active Hives

Free: up to 3 Hives. Premium: unlimited. Same gate as books — natural upgrade trigger when a writer is actively collaborating on many projects.

2

Larger collaborator teams

Free: up to 5 members per Hive. Premium: unlimited members. Scales well — most hobbyist Hives stay small.

3

Advanced roles & permissions

Free: Owner / Contributor only. Premium: custom roles (Editor, Beta Reader, Proofreader) with granular permissions per chapter.

4

Hive analytics & contribution stats

Per-member word count contributions, activity heatmaps, chapter completion velocity. Free users just see basic activity.

5

Collaborative export

Free: only the Hive owner can export. Premium: any premium member can export the shared project in EPUB/PDF/DOCX.

6

Hive version history

Track the full edit history of a shared chapter — who changed what, when. Restore any version. Premium only (mirrors solo version history gate).

Free Hive always includes: creating/joining Hives, inviting collaborators, chapter submissions & review, inline comments, suggestions, wiki, outline board, buzz feed.
