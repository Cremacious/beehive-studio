SaaS Pricing Model

What's the gate between free and paid? This determines what drives upgrades.

A

Projects / storage limit

Free: 1–2 active projects, limited storage. Paid: unlimited projects + cloud backup. Writers hit the wall naturally as they start new books. Low friction for new users, clear upgrade moment.

B

Collaboration is paid

Free: solo writing only. Paid: invite co-authors, beta readers, editors. Collab is the power feature — solo writers get full value free, teams pay. Mirrors how Linear, Figma, and Notion grew.

C

Export is paid

Free: write forever, export never (or only plain text). Paid: EPUB, DOCX, PDF export. Already proven in v1. Low barrier to start, upgrade when you need to publish. Simple to enforce.

D

Combination gate

Free: 3 projects + plain text export + solo only. Paid: unlimited projects + all export formats + collaboration. Multiple upgrade triggers. More complex to communicate but maximizes conversion paths.
