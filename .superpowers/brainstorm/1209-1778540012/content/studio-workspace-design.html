What's in each panel

BINDER (left)
  • Full manuscript tree (parts → chapters)
  • Front matter & back matter
  • Research folder (characters, world notes, outline, references)
  • Drag to reorder chapters
EDITOR (center)
  • TipTap rich text (same as v1)
  • Formatting toolbar (bold, italic, headings, scene breaks)
  • Focus mode hides all panels (premium)
  • Inline comments from Hive collaborators
INSPECTOR (right)
  • Chapter & book word counts
  • Chapter status (Idea / Outline / Draft / Revised / Final)
  • Daily writing goal + streak
  • Chapter notes (private scratchpad)
  • Publishing metadata (ISBN, genre, trim size) — collapses here
