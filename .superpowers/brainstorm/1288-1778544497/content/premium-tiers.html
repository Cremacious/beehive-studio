Design Section 3 — Free vs Premium Tiers

Everything we decided, consolidated. Does this feel fair to users and compelling enough to upgrade?

🐝 Free
Supported by ads. Full writing tool — never a crippled demo.
STUDIO
✓ Up to 3 active books
✓ Full TipTap writing editor
✓ Binder, Editor, Inspector panels
✓ Chapter status tracking
✓ Basic word count & goals
✓ Plain text export
HIVE (COLLABORATION)
✓ Up to 3 active Hives
✓ Up to 5 members per Hive
✓ Chapter submissions & review
✓ Inline comments & suggestions
✓ Wiki, outline board, buzz feed
✓ Owner & Contributor roles
COMMUNITY & DISCOVER
✓ Publish to Discover
✓ Follow authors, likes, comments
✓ Reading shelf & bookmarks
✓ Sparks writing contests
✓ Author profile page
PREMIUM
✦ Beehive Premium
No ads. Unlock the full hive. ~$X/month.
STUDIO — EVERYTHING FREE +
✦ Unlimited active books
✦ Export: EPUB, PDF, DOCX
✦ Version history & snapshots
✦ Advanced goals, streaks & analytics
✦ Genre-specific templates (thriller, romance…)
✦ Focus / distraction-free mode
✦ Full data export / project archive (ZIP)
✦ Publishing prep tools (manuscript format, KDP, IngramSpark presets)
HIVE — EVERYTHING FREE +
✦ Unlimited active Hives
✦ Unlimited members per Hive
✦ Advanced roles (Editor, Beta Reader, Proofreader)
✦ Hive contribution analytics
✦ Collaborative export (any premium member)
✦ Hive version history
EVERYWHERE
✦ No ads — ever
✦ Premium badge on profile
THE UPGRADE TRIGGERS — moments a free user hits a wall and considers upgrading
📚 Creates their 4th book → "Unlimited books with Premium"
📤 Tries to export → "EPUB/PDF/DOCX export is Premium"
🔄 Wants to restore a draft → "Version history is Premium"
🐝 Creates their 4th Hive → "Unlimited Hives with Premium"
👥 Invites a 6th Hive member → "Larger teams with Premium"
🎯 Wants pace projections → "Advanced analytics is Premium"

Does this tier split feel fair and compelling? Anything to add, move, or reconsider?
