Glass card + Bento grid

Modal: a polished radial-glow header, iOS segmented control, recessed textarea, brand-yellow primary. Feed: varied-size tiles in a 6-col bento grid — featured posts span wide, links get cover treatment, text posts pack tight. Most "Apple-like".

Option B

Sticky notes on a corkboard

Modal: pick a post type as a tile-card, then write on a virtual sticky note. Feed: every buzz is a tinted sticky pinned to a subtle dot-grid corkboard, rotated slightly for that human-scrawled feel. Most playful — leans into "writers' bulletin board".

Command-bar compose + Masonry feed
