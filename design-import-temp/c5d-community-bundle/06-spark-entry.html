To the lighthouse keeper

VN
Vesper Nox
@vesper.nox · 2 days ago

I never learned your name, only the rhythm of your light — three counts dark, one count bright — the way you keep telling the whole black coast that someone is still awake out there, still minding the rocks. I used to set my breathing to it on the nights I couldn't sleep.

You won't remember the summer the ferry broke down and forty of us slept on the chapel floor. You brought up tea at two in the morning, said nothing, set it down, left. I have thought about that cup more than I have thought about most of the people I'm supposed to love.

I'm writing because the lighthouse is automated now. They told me last week, the way you tell someone a distant relative has died — gently, and too late to do anything about it. No more keeper. Just a bulb and a timer and the same patient three-and-one, minding rocks that no longer need a person to care about them.

So this is the thing I never said: thank you for being awake. I am going to keep setting my breathing to that light for as long as it turns, and I am not going to tell anyone why.
