About this club

We read one mystery or thriller a month and meet in the discussions to untangle it together. Spoiler-tagged threads, no gatekeeping, and a soft spot for an unreliable narrator. New members always welcome.

House rules

1. Tag spoilers past the current chapter.
2. One discussion thread per chapter — keep it tidy.
3. Disagree with the book, never the reader.
4. Finished early? The queue is open for suggestions.
