Sable Thornwax

Character vault hive protagonist

A real person, animal, or being who shapes the story.

Disposition

Wry, stubborn, allergic to ceremony. She trusts the work of her hands long before she trusts a person — which is precisely the wound the found family of keepers and smugglers will spend three books prying open.

She still hums the binding song when she thinks no one is listening. She is almost always wrong about who is listening.

Background

Born to the lower-comb keepers in the third season of the Long Hum. Apprenticed at eleven to Warden Pell. Lost the veil in the Silent Court hearing of season twenty-two.

Notes

Possible reveal in book 3: she was the one who taught the comb to sing — the Court “silenced” her because of this.
