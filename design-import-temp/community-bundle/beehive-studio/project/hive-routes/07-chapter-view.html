The Comb Cathedral

Chapter from The Lost Hive Chronicles by @alex.writes

Owner

Metadata

Revised

Sable returns to the cathedral she was exiled from, only to find the comb humming a song that should be dead. A reunion, a theft, and the first hint that the Silencing has a hand behind it.

Scene Planner (click to expand)
Goal

Get Sable back inside the Vault hive without being recognised by the Court wardens.

Conflict

The warden on the gate is Mira — the friend who testified against her.

Outcome

She gets in, but Mira lets her — which means Mira wants something, and that debt will come due.

Body

The gate to the Vault hive had not changed in eleven seasons, which was its own kind of insult. Same wax-sealed hinges, same smell of smoke and old royal jelly, same warden’s lamp throwing its amber circle on the comb.

Sable pulled the borrowed veil lower and stepped into the light.

“Name,” the warden said, not looking up. Then she did look up, and the lamp shook in her hand. “Oh,” said Mira. “Oh, you absolute fool.”

Beneath them, impossibly, the comb began to sing.
