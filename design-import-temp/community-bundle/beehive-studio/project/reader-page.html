When the last great hive falls silent and the queens begin to vanish one by one, a disgraced apiarist named Sable must descend into the wax cathedrals beneath the city to uncover who — or what — is unmaking the swarm. Part political thriller, part coming-of-age, The Lost Hive Chronicles follows a found family of keepers, smugglers, and exiled royals as they race to restore the song that holds their world together before the silence spreads beyond the comb.
